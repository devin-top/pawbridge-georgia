Frequently asked questions

A few practical answers about PawBridge and getting in touch.

Is PawBridge an animal shelter?

No. PawBridge is an independent technology and creative outreach initiative, not a shelter or adoption authority.

Are animals available for adoption on this site?

No. The animal imagery and campaign designs are illustrative examples. No active animal listings or verified shelter partnerships are claimed.

Can rescue teams request support?

Yes. Contact neostorys@gmail.com or use the contact form on the home page. Requests are evaluated individually.

Is the future AI-powered platform ready?

Not yet. The current site is a static prototype. Features such as shelter dashboards and animal listings are proposed development work.

What if the contact form fails?

Send an email directly to neostorys@gmail.com. The form's delivery depends on the Netlify Forms configuration of the deployed site.
