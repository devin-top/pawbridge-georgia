Privacy notice

A simple overview of how PawBridge handles contact requests.

Information you send

If you submit an inquiry, the form asks for your name, email address, request category and message. Please avoid sending sensitive personal data.

Purpose and service provider

Inquiry details are used to read and respond to your message. When Netlify Forms is enabled, submissions are processed and stored by Netlify. You may also choose to send an email using your own email provider.

Retention and requests

Contact data should only be kept as long as needed for inquiries and related correspondence. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact neostorys@gmail.com.

External resources

This website uses Google Fonts and may link to external websites. Their services may process visitor data under their own policies. This notice describes the current prototype and should be reviewed before any expanded platform launch.
